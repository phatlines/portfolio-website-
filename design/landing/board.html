    System

    Type scale

    Anybody at 88% width and weight 750, set in mixed case, is the display voice. It's self-hosted in /public/fonts. Hanken stays for text. The system mono is for dates, rates and URLs only. Display tops out at 96px and nothing goes below 15px.

    Colour tokens

    Restrained: a white page and near-black ink, with one circuit-board green that does three jobs (the primary action, availability and active states). A pale stage grey sits behind devices to give the screenshots a surface. Project colours appear only inside their own screens.

    Spacing scale

    A 4px base. Section padding becomes a fixed token instead of 12–14vh, which grew to about 150px on tall screens.

    Background system: page, stage, screens

    This replaces v1's contour shader, grain and colour changes. The depth comes from the work itself: real screens in device frames, sitting on a pale stage, on a white page. It needs no WebGL, so the OGL Backdrop is removed. Lenis stays.

    L0 · page (CSS)White #fff. Clients and recruiters read it at a desk in daylight, often beside a brief or a job spec.
    L1 · stage (CSS)A pale grey #eef0ed panel with an 8px grid at 5% ink. It sits only behind device stages and the process diagram, where something has been built.
    L2 · devices (CSS)Browser, phone and Mac frames with a real two-layer shadow (offset and blur). This is the only elevated layer on the page.
    L3 · screens (media)The real screenshots and loops. They're the only colour on the page apart from the green.
    Removed The OGL contour Backdrop, Grain, data-paint colour changes, the letter-rain and clap openings, Gebuk, and v2's datasheet parts (MA-01, pins, running heads).
